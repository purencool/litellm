Structural Planning & Comparisons
The choice of building method significantly impacts your total budget, construction timeline, and design flexibility. The table below compares the three primary approaches to building a home:
Building Method	Average Timeline	Primary Advantages	Key Trade-offs
Custom Build	10–16+ months	Unlimited design freedom; tailored exactly to your lifestyle.	Highest cost per square metre; longest timeline.
Production / Volume Build	6–10 months	Cost-effective; predictable pricing; pre-designed floor plans.	Limited customization; identical houses in the area.
Prefab / Modular Build	3–6 months	Fast construction; built in climate-controlled factories.	High upfront delivery fees; limited site adaptability.
